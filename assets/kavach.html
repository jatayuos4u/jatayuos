✅ Safe Digital Practices

Follow these golden rules to stay safe online

🔐
Password Safety
  • Use 12+ character passwords with mixed case, numbers, symbols
  • Never reuse passwords across accounts
  • Enable 2-Factor Authentication (2FA) everywhere
  • Use a password manager like KeePassXC
  • Never write passwords in notebooks or phone notes
📱
UPI & Payment Safety
  • NEVER enter UPI PIN to RECEIVE money
  • Verify recipient name before sending
  • Use only official apps (BHIM, GPay, PhonePe, Paytm)
  • Don't scan QR codes from strangers
  • Enable transaction alerts on your bank
📞
Call & SMS Safety
  • Bank will NEVER call asking for OTP/PIN/CVV
  • Police/CBI will NEVER call for "digital arrest"
  • Verify caller ID independently (search official number)
  • Don't install AnyDesk/TeamViewer on caller's advice
  • Hang up on suspicious calls immediately
🌐
Internet Safety
  • Check for HTTPS (🔒) before entering data
  • Don't click suspicious links in SMS/email
  • Avoid public Wi-Fi for banking
  • Keep browser and OS updated
  • Use ad-blockers to avoid malvertising
📧
Email Safety
  • Check sender email carefully (look for misspellings)
  • Don't open attachments from unknown senders
  • Beware of "urgent" or "threatening" language
  • Never click "unsubscribe" in spam emails
  • Verify lottery/prize emails - they're ALWAYS fake
📲
App Safety
  • Download apps only from official stores
  • Check app permissions before installing
  • Read reviews and download count
  • Avoid "instant loan" apps with high interest
  • Uninstall apps you don't use regularly
🆔
Identity Protection
  • Don't share Aadhaar/PAN photos on social media
  • Lock your Aadhaar biometrics on UIDAI portal
  • Shred documents with personal info before discarding
  • Monitor your credit report annually (CIBIL)
  • Report lost ID documents immediately
👨‍👩‍👧
Family Safety
  • Educate elderly family about phone scams
  • Set up parental controls for children
  • Teach kids not to share personal info online
  • Monitor children's social media activity
  • Create family emergency plan for cyber incidents

📞 Report Cyber Crime

If you've been scammed, act FAST. The first hour is critical.

⏰ Golden Hour Rule

Report within 1 hour of fraud for best chance of fund recovery

📞
Step 1: Call Immediately
  • 1930 - National Cyber Crime Helpline
  • 112 - National Emergency
  • 14400 - Banking Fraud Helpline
  • Have transaction details ready
  • Note the complaint reference number
🏦
Step 2: Contact Your Bank
  • Call bank's fraud department immediately
  • Freeze compromised cards/accounts
  • Request transaction reversal
  • Get written acknowledgment
  • Change net banking password
🌐
Step 3: File Online Complaint
  • Visit cybercrime.gov.in
  • Register with mobile number
  • File detailed complaint with evidence
  • Upload screenshots, SMS, call logs
  • Track status using ACK number
👮
Step 4: Visit Police Station
  • Visit local police/Cyber Cell
  • Carry printed complaint + evidence
  • Get FIR registered (mandatory for >₹10,000)
  • Collect FIR copy for bank/insurance
  • Follow up regularly

📄 Documents to Keep Ready

  • Bank account statement showing fraudulent transaction
  • Screenshots of suspicious SMS/emails/WhatsApp
  • Call logs with scammer's number
  • Transaction reference numbers (UTR/RR Number)
  • Aadhaar/PAN card (for identity verification)
  • Any communication with the scammer
